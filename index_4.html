About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Sookmyung Women's University (since March 2026). My research sits at the intersection of pure mathematics and applied AI, with a primary focus on Homogeneous Dynamics, Topological Data Analysis (TDA), and Graph Neural Networks / Graph Transformers.

I am particularly interested in bringing rigorous mathematical foundations to Electronic Design Automation (EDA) and semiconductor AI, as well as Medical Data Science. Prior to joining Sookmyung, I was a Staff Engineer at Samsung Electronics AI Center researching AI for semiconductor design and verification.
